instruction to publish. Use them after MVP closeout or for an explicitly

## Captions and voiceover

Use captions even if the video has voiceover. Many viewers watch short videos
muted. Keep text short and avoid filling the frame with paragraphs.

Suggested on-screen captions:

```text
Public core
Private overlays
Release gates
Verifiable automation
```

Suggested call-to-action:

```text
GitHub: github.com/yiheng8023/open-resource-governance
Project: YIYUAN Meridian / 易元经纬
```

## Music guidance

Background music is optional. If used, keep it low-volume and non-distracting.

Recommended style:

- minimal ambient electronic;
- soft lo-fi instrumental;
- light documentary synth bed;
- no vocals;
- no copyrighted commercial tracks;
- no dramatic cyber-action music.

Use royalty-free or platform-provided music libraries, and keep the license
record with the final video export. If the music source is uncertain, publish
without music.

## Logo and trademark guidance

The current assets use a text wordmark only. Do not use `®` or imply registered
trademark status unless registration is complete. If the owner later provides a
project logo, add it only after confirming it is intended for public project
use.

Safe wording:

```text
YIYUAN Meridian
YIYUAN Meridian / 易元经纬
```

Avoid:

```text
YIYUAN Meridian®
Official certified platform
```

## Public-safety checks

Before using or regenerating assets, confirm screenshots do not reveal:

- private repositories;
- private browser bookmarks;
- local machine paths;
- account menus or private messages;
- tokens, cookies, OAuth material, credentials, or emails other than the
  intentional public contact email;
- private issues, PRs, Actions logs, or raw conversations.
